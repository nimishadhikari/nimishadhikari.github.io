I am an Postdoctoral Reseach fellow at Brigham and Women's Hospital and Harvard medical school. I also am a Biostatistician at the VA Boston Healthcare System. I did my PhD at Boston University, and my dissertation advisor was Dr. Gina Peloso.

My research is focused on genetic and multi-omic analysis of large scale Electronic Health Record (EHR) data. I have experience in designing and performing analysis for studies on topics like Genome Wide Association Studies (GWAS), Mendelian Randomization (MR), Bayesian methods for genetic outcomes and genetic determinanants for disease progression. Currently I am interested in using Machine Learning and Deep Learning methods to encode EHR data, and using these encodings to understand novel biology. I also currently work on various studies with the Million Veteran Program (MVP), a large dataset of a million veterans, where I have studied genetic determinanants for phenotypes including incident and subsequent stroke, lymphoedema, and drug responses (BMI post-GLP1).
